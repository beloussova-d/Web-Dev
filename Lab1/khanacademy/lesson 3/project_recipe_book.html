Noodle Soup

  • Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: 1 serving
Ingredients Quantity
Noodles 1 serving
Green onion 2 tablespoons
Cilantro 2 tablespoons
White pepper 1/2 tsp
Dashi powder 1 tsp
Soy sauce 1.5 tablespoon
Sesame oil 1 tablespoon
Noodle water 2 cups

Step 1: Prepare noodles based on instructions, and save 2-3 cups of noodle water

Step 2: Add green onion, cilantro, white pepper, dashi powder, soy sauce, and sesame oil in a bowl.

Step 3: Pour hot noodle water into the bowl, and add in the noodles.

Step 4: Optional, finish off with a drizzle of chili oil.

Source: Easy Noodle Soup (10 Minutes)
